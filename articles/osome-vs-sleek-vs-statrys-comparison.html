Osome vs Sleek vs Statrys: Hong Kong Company Formation Compared (2026)

Quick Answer: Choose Osome if you prioritise a digital-first workflow and a published lower starting price. Choose Sleek if you value a broader public review footprint and an all-in-one formation offer. Choose Statrys if multi-currency business payments are central to your operating model. These are fit-based conclusions, not universal rankings. None of the three can guarantee bank or payment-account approval, and all should be verified for TCSP licence status before signing.

Four questions founders actually ask

Which provider has the lowest published starting price for Hong Kong formation?

Generally, Sleek publishes the lowest formation-only starting figure among the three in this dated comparison (HK$1,545 for the basic formation entry, verified 10 September 2026). Osome's published Hong Kong pricing shows Essential at HK$4,650 and Comprehensive at HK$8,950, with promotional figures at HK$6,195 on the same pricing page. Statrys positions itself around a multi-currency business account bundle rather than a single formation price. A headline price is not a complete first-year cost — compare government charges, registered address, Annual Return, accounting, audit, tax, optional items and renewal fees before deciding.

Which provider is best for multi-currency business payments?

Generally, Statrys is the most relevant option to investigate when multi-currency receipts, payments or transfers are central to the business model. Statrys combines company formation information with a multi-currency business account and bookkeeping. That does not guarantee eligibility or approval — confirm the payment product, supported currencies, transfer fees, safeguarding arrangements and the institution's own due-diligence requirements.

Are any of these providers licensed under Hong Kong's TCSP regime?

Generally yes, where the work involves company secretarial duties. Company secretarial work in Hong Kong requires a Trust or Company Service Provider (TCSP) licence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap 615). Verify each provider's legal entity on the Companies Registry TCSP search before signing any engagement letter. None of the three providers' published pages state a TCSP licence number directly; verification is on the reader.

Does any provider guarantee bank or payment-account approval?

No. A provider may help prepare documents, review information or make an introduction, but a bank, neobank or payment institution makes its own risk and customer-due-diligence decision. Approval can depend on ownership, business activity, countries served, source of funds and expected transaction patterns. Treat any banking or payment support as assistance, not as a promise.

How was this comparison prepared?

This review compares official provider pages, published package information, publicly visible review evidence and TCSP licensing references. Review content is directional rather than representative: it cannot establish approval rates, average satisfaction, service quality or the experience of every customer. Provider pages and price states were checked on 10 September 2026.

Prices, promotions, review counts and package inclusions change. A headline price may exclude government charges, renewals, optional work or services performed by a separately regulated professional. Ask each provider for a written scope and renewal schedule before paying.

How should founders compare Osome, Sleek and Statrys?

Compare three things in order: evidence traceability, scope clarity and operational fit. Evidence traceability means you can open the source behind a claim. Scope clarity means you understand first-year work, renewals, exclusions and professional responsibility. Operational fit means the provider suits your company's banking, accounting, compliance, formation and communication needs.

A low advertised price is not automatically the lowest first-year cost. A high review count is not proof of consistent service. A payment account is not the same as a bank account, and no company-services provider can guarantee approval by a bank or payment institution.

What is the McKenzie fit framework?

The framework avoids an invented star ranking. Ask whether the provider gives you traceable evidence, a clear scope and a workable operating relationship. A founder who needs tax advice, audit sign-off or regulated payment services should identify the actual professional or institution responsible for that work, rather than assuming the platform handles everything itself.

What does evidence traceability mean?

Open the underlying pricing page, service page, licence record or review permalink. Record the date checked. For reviews, note that an individual account describes one customer's experience, not the provider's average outcome. For licensing, use the Companies Registry TCSP licence search where a TCSP check is relevant.

What does scope clarity mean?

Ask for one document showing the contracting entity, formation work, registered address, company-secretarial duties, accounting period, audit arrangement, tax-filing responsibility, government charges, renewal fees, optional services and support route. If the quotation does not separate one-off and recurring items, the comparison is incomplete.

What does operational fit mean?

Choose the workflow you can actually use. Some founders want a local relationship that coordinates several workstreams. Others prefer online onboarding and published plan architecture. A founder whose main need is multi-currency payment functionality should also test eligibility, transfer terms and the institution's own due-diligence process.

Founder pre-purchase workflow — five steps

Use this five-step workflow to convert the comparison above into a decision you can defend to a co-founder, an investor or your own accountant.

Step 1 — Read each provider's published scope

Open Osome's Hong Kong pricing page, Sleek's Hong Kong pricing page and Statrys's Hong Kong business account page side by side. Note package names, listed inclusions, exclusions, and any promotional prices. Note the page date if shown.

Step 2 — Check TCSP licence status

Company secretarial work in Hong Kong requires a Trust or Company Service Provider (TCSP) licence under Cap 615. Open the Companies Registry TCSP search, look up the legal entity behind each provider and confirm an active licence is in place. None of the three providers' published pages state a TCSP licence number directly.

Step 3 — Request a dated written quotation

Email all three providers and request a single dated quotation that lists: (a) government charges, (b) first-year professional fees, (c) registered-address scope and term, (d) Annual Return handling, (e) accounting, (f) tax filing, (g) audit coordination and (h) renewal pricing for year two onwards.

Step 4 — Map the workstreams to responsible professionals

For each workstream (formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax, payment support), identify the legal entity and the responsible professional. A separate regulated professional — for example a HK practising CPA — may handle the audit or tax filing. Ask each provider to confirm in writing.

Step 5 — Match the provider to your founder situation

If you want a digital-first workflow with a published lower starting price, Osome may fit. If you want a broader formation package with a larger public review footprint, Sleek may fit. If multi-currency payments are central to your operating model, Statrys may fit. Pause any choice if your company has complex cross-border tax, regulated activities, unusual ownership, high-risk jurisdictions or a difficult banking profile.

What should you know about Osome?

Osome presents a digital-first Hong Kong company-formation and accounting workflow. Its published Hong Kong pricing page (verified 10 September 2026) shows Essential at HK$4,650, Comprehensive at HK$8,950, and an Ecommerce Comprehensive tier with promotional figures also displayed. Treat these as dated published prices, not a guaranteed final quotation.

Osome may fit a founder who wants a structured online process and can manage a checklist of add-ons and renewals. Before appointment, confirm the registered-address period, Annual Return coverage, accounting scope, tax filing, audit responsibility, document retention and escalation route. A digital interface may simplify onboarding, but it does not remove the need to understand who performs each professional service.

These accounts are useful prompts for questions about response times, escalation and continuity. They do not establish a provider-wide service result, and they should not be converted into a satisfaction percentage.

What should you know about Sleek?

Sleek is positioned as a broader formation and corporate-services provider with a substantial public review presence. The Sleek Hong Kong pricing page (verified 10 September 2026) lists a formation entry from HK$1,545 and accounting packages ranging from HK$4,973 to HK$13,973 depending on monthly expense volume and service tier. Treat these as dated published prices, not a guaranteed final quotation.

Sleek may fit a founder who wants an all-in-one formation package and values a larger public review footprint as one research signal. Confirm the appointed service entity, TCSP position where relevant, registered-address scope, renewal price, ad-hoc change charges, accounting and audit boundaries, tax-filing responsibility and how refunds or disputes are escalated.

What should you verify about Sleek's licence position?

Where a service involves company-secretarial or TCSP work, verify the relevant legal entity and licence status through the official Companies Registry search. A licence check supports regulatory due diligence; it does not guarantee response quality, pricing, continuity or a particular customer outcome.

What should you know about Statrys?

Statrys combines company-formation information with a multi-currency business account, corporate services and bookkeeping. The Statrys HK page (verified 10 September 2026) is positioned around a single bundled offering rather than separate formation-only and accounting-only packages. That may be attractive to a founder whose daily operations involve receiving or sending several currencies.

It also creates a sharper boundary to check: a payment account or payment service is not automatically a bank account, and eligibility remains subject to the institution's own onboarding and risk decisions. A payment-institution e-money account does not enjoy the same statutory deposit protection as a licensed bank deposit under the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Scheme. Read the safeguarding statement before paying in funds.

What did the two sampled Statrys reviews indicate?

The selected sample includes one account describing clear fees or responsive support and one account raising a transfer-service concern. These are individual observations, not an approval rate or a general finding about every payment or formation customer. Use them to test the provider's support, complaints and transfer processes before signing.

Which provider fits different founder situations?

When might Osome be the better fit?

  • You prefer digital onboarding and a platform-led workflow.
  • You want to compare a published lower starting price.
  • You are comfortable checking registered-address, accounting, tax, audit and renewal add-ons.
  • You can obtain written confirmation of the support and escalation route.

When might Sleek be the better fit?

  • You value a broader formation package and a large public review footprint as one signal.
  • You want to compare company-service coverage beyond basic incorporation.
  • You are prepared to verify renewal pricing, change fees and the responsible professional.
  • You want to understand how refunds, handover and service disputes are handled.

When might Statrys be the better fit?

  • Multi-currency receipts, payments or transfers are central to the business.
  • You are willing to complete the institution's own eligibility and due-diligence process.
  • You want to separate company formation from payment-account approval in your planning.
  • You will compare transfer fees, supported currencies, safeguarding and service boundaries.

When should you pause before choosing any of them?

Pause if your company has complex cross-border tax, regulated activities, unusual ownership, high-risk jurisdictions, material intellectual property or difficult banking facts. These providers may help with formation or administration, but they do not replace qualified legal, tax, audit or regulated financial advice where the risk is material.

There is no universal winner. Osome is the clearest fit for a founder prioritising a digital-first workflow and published plan structure. Sleek is worth shortlisting where a broader formation package and public review footprint matter. Statrys is the most relevant option to investigate where multi-currency payments are a core operating requirement.
