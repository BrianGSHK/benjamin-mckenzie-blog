Osome vs Sleek vs Statrys: Hong Kong Company Formation Compared (2026)

An honest, review-based comparison of the three most popular digital company formation services in Hong Kong. Based on real online reviews and verified pricing.

Aug 11, 2026 · 10 min read

Sleek has the highest Trustpilot rating (4.2/5) with over 3,700 reviews, Osome offers the lowest starting price at HK$6,195, and Statrys uniquely combines company formation with multi-currency business banking. Prices and review counts are time-sensitive; verify them directly with each provider before relying on them. All three are legitimate, regulated service providers — but they differ significantly in pricing, customer support quality, and included services. For a complete walkthrough of the registration process, see our guide.

Quick Answer: Choose Sleek for the most proven track record (3,700+ reviews). Choose Osome for the lowest starting price (HK$6,195). Choose Statrys if you also need a multi-currency business account. All three are legitimate and regulated.

Formation providers do not replace professional accounting and tax advice. For a separate reference point, see KPMG’s Hong Kong tax services overview, which describes tax compliance issues businesses may need to manage after incorporation.

Direct Answers

How should you compare Osome, Sleek, and Statrys?

Osome, Sleek, and Statrys should be compared by total first-year cost, included registered-office and secretary services, accounting and tax scope, banking features, support channels, cancellation terms, and licensing or regulatory information. Published prices and review metrics change, so buyers should verify current package details directly and compare like-for-like inclusions before choosing.

Which Hong Kong company formation service is cheapest?

There is no permanently cheapest provider because packages, promotions, government charges, renewal terms, and optional services change. Osome, Sleek, or Statrys may show different headline prices that exclude registered addresses, secretary renewals, accounting, tax, or banking. Compare the complete first-year and renewal totals rather than relying on a starting price alone.

What should founders check before choosing a company formation provider?

Founders should verify the provider’s legal identity, Hong Kong registered-office and company-secretary arrangements, relevant licences where applicable, scope of work, refund and renewal terms, data handling, support process, and current customer feedback. They should also confirm who handles Annual Returns, business registration, accounting, audit coordination, and tax filings after incorporation.

Osome: Detailed Review

Overview

Osome is a fintech platform founded in 2017, headquartered in Singapore. It automates financial administrative tasks for entrepreneurs, offering incorporation, company secretary, accounting, and tax services in Hong Kong, Singapore, and the UK.

Pricing (2026)

  • Essential Package: HK$6,195 (excludes registered address)
  • Company Secretary: Included in packages
  • Registered Address: Additional cost for Essential plan
  • Annual Return: Included

What Osome customers say about service

The review record is mixed rather than uniformly positive. One 4-star reviewer, Stan Karpenko, described a dedicated account manager, timely accounting and replies within a few hours. By contrast, Annabel Law’s 1-star review described a two-month onboarding delay, no account manager and difficulty reaching the support line. Read the 4-star review and 1-star review in full.

Sleek: Detailed Review

Overview

Sleek is a B Corp certified company holding TCSP licence TC006483. The Hong Kong TCSP licensee search can be used to verify licensed trust or company service providers. It's listed on the Financial Times High-Growth Companies Asia-Pacific 2026 ranking. Sleek offers incorporation, company secretary, accounting, and banking partnerships in Hong Kong and Singapore.

Pricing (2026)

  • Incorporation (Foreigners): From HK$6,973
  • Company Secretary: Included in all plans
  • Registered Address: Included (HK$2,000/year value)
  • Annual Return: Worth HK$105 — included (see our Annual Compliance Guide)
  • Business Account: Free with Aspire or Airwallex (subject to approval)
  • Ad-hoc Changes: HK$2,500 per share or director change (Standard tier)

Statrys: Detailed Review

Overview

Statrys is a licensed Money Service Operator in Hong Kong and Major Payment Institution in Singapore (MAS licensed). It uniquely combines company formation with multi-currency business banking, making it a one-stop solution for international businesses.

Pricing (2026)

  • Government Fees: HK$3,895 (HK$1,545 Companies Registry + HK$2,350 Business Registration)
  • Total Service Cost: Average HK$9,474 (based on survey of 512 entrepreneurs)
  • Company Secretary: Included
  • Registered Address: Included
  • Business Account: Statrys Business Account included

What Statrys customers say about onboarding and support

Statrys reviews also show contrasting experiences. Andrew’s verified 4-star review praised a fast online setup, clear fees and responsive support, while a 1-star review from a customer described a funds-transfer review as “very bad service”. ⚠️ Important Note: Statrys is NOT a bank. It holds an MSO (Money Service Operator) licence, not a banking licence. This means client funds are NOT protected by the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Scheme (which covers up to HK$500,000 at licensed banks). MSO funds are subject to different regulatory requirements than bank deposits.

Concerns About Dual Licensing (MSO + TCSP)

Statrys holds both an MSO licence (for banking/payments) and a TCSP licence (for company secretary services). While this is legal, there are several concerns to consider:

  • Different Regulators: MSO is regulated by Hong Kong Customs and Excise Department. TCSP is regulated by the Companies Registry. Dual licensing means oversight by two different bodies.
  • Client Fund Segregation: As an MSO (not a bank), Statrys is not required to segregate client funds in the same way banks do. In case of financial difficulties, client funds may be at higher risk compared to bank deposits.
  • Conflict of Interest: Providing both banking (MSO) and company secretary (TCSP) services creates potential conflicts. For example, if Statrys freezes your MSO account, it could also affect your company secretary relationship.
  • No Deposit Protection: Unlike bank deposits covered by the Deposit Protection Scheme (up to HK$500,000), MSO-held funds have no government-backed protection.
  • AML/CFT Compliance: Statrys must comply with both MSO and TCSP anti-money laundering requirements, which adds complexity. Any compliance failure could affect both services.

⚠️ Risk Assessment: If you keep significant funds in a Statrys account, understand that these funds are NOT bank deposits. They are subject to MSO regulations, not banking regulations. In a worst-case scenario (e.g., Statrys insolvency), recovery of funds may be more difficult than with a licensed bank.

Who Should Choose Each Service?

Choose Osome If:

  • You want the lowest starting price
  • You're comfortable with automated/bot support
  • You don't need a registered address in the Essential plan
  • You're setting up in Singapore or UK as well

Choose Sleek If:

  • You want the most proven service (3,700+ reviews)
  • You value B Corp certification and social responsibility
  • You want a free business account with Aspire or Airwallex
  • You want all-inclusive pricing (address included)

Choose Statrys If:

  • You need multi-currency business banking as a core feature
  • You want a single platform for company + banking
  • You're an international business with cross-border transactions
  • You value regulatory licensing (MSO licence in HK)
  • ⚠️ Understand: Statrys is an MSO, not a bank. Client funds are NOT covered by the Deposit Protection Scheme.

The Bottom Line

Our Assessment: All three services are legitimate and regulated. For pure company formation, Sleek offers the best balance of price, reviews, and included services. For businesses that also need banking, Statrys provides a unique integrated solution. Osome is the budget option but be prepared for automated support.

Important Note: This comparison is based on publicly available online reviews and pricing as of August 2026. Prices and services may change. Always verify current pricing directly with each provider before making a decision.
