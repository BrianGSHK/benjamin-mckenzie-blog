Quick Answer: GetStarted.hk may be the better fit if you want a broader Hong Kong corporate-services relationship covering formation, secretarial work, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance. Statrys may suit founders who prefer a digital-first workflow with a multi-currency business-account platform and published Hong Kong plans. Neither headline price, review score nor licensing status guarantees service quality, approval or tax results. The right question is which provider's scope matches your company's actual needs, then verify the licence and the written quotation.

Four questions founders actually ask

Which provider is cheaper for a basic Hong Kong incorporation?

Generally, GetStarted.hk's Residents Exclusive package (HK$5,650) undercuts Statrys' promotional price (HK$7,875) for a basic incorporation, and both include government fees of approximately HK$3,895. However, neither headline price tells you the full first-year cost. Annual-return work, accounting, tax filing and renewal pricing may sit outside the published figure.

Which provider is broader in service scope?

Generally, GetStarted.hk, based on its published formation page and accounting page. Its service catalogue explicitly covers formation, company secretarial, registered address, accounting, audit coordination, tax filing and banking-related assistance as ongoing workstreams — not just formation.

Which provider is better for a non-resident founder?

Generally, the choice depends on your banking profile and the jurisdictions you serve, not the provider alone. A non-resident founder typically needs a registered-address service, certified document handling and a higher-touch banking introduction. Both providers list support for these; verify what is included, what costs extra and what the bank's own due-diligence process looks like.

Is either provider regulated as a TCSP in Hong Kong?

Statrys confirms its TCSP licence (TC008677) on its Hong Kong company-registration page. GetStarted.hk doesn't state its licence number directly on the formation page, but provides company-secretarial services and holds a TCSP arrangement for Hong Kong secretarial work. Verify both providers' current licence status through the Companies Registry TCSP search before signing.

What is the main difference between GetStarted.hk and Statrys?

GetStarted.hk is positioned around a broader Hong Kong corporate-services relationship, while Statrys is positioned around a digital-first platform with an integrated multi-currency business account. The practical choice is therefore less about finding a universal winner and more about matching the provider's scope, communication model and professional responsibilities to your company's needs.

Both providers advertise formation and ongoing corporate support. Their published pages do not, by themselves, prove that every service is delivered by the same legal entity, that every item is included in every package or that a bank will approve an account. Those points belong in your written pre-purchase checklist.

How should a founder compare the two providers?

Use three tests: evidence traceability, scope clarity and operational fit. Evidence traceability asks whether you can open the underlying provider page or document. Scope clarity asks what is included now, what renews later and which professional is responsible. Operational fit asks whether you need a relationship-led service or a more structured online workflow.

Which provider offers the broader relationship?

GetStarted.hk may suit a founder who wants several Hong Kong administrative workstreams coordinated through one local relationship. Its published materials cover formation and a wider range of secretarial, accounting, audit, tax and banking-related assistance. Confirm the exact service entity, licence position and professional scope before relying on that breadth.

Which provider offers the more digital-first experience?

Statrys may suit a founder who prefers online onboarding, a clear published package price and a platform-led workflow. That can make initial comparison easier, but a digital interface does not remove the need to verify the responsible entity, annual-return coverage, tax or audit scope, document retention and escalation route.

What does GetStarted.hk publish?

The official GetStarted.hk formation page lists four Hong Kong packages: Residents Exclusive at HK$5,650, Deluxe at HK$7,700, E-Commerce Starter at HK$12,480 and Premium Trading at HK$19,545. These figures are a dated snapshot, not a permanent price promise. Ask which government charges, registered-address services, annual-return work and renewals are included.

The provider's wider positioning may be useful where a founder expects to need company secretarial support, accounting coordination, tax-return preparation, audit preparation or banking assistance after incorporation. Review the accounting-services scope and request a written schedule separating recurring services from one-off work.

GetStarted.hk also advertises that it doesn't charge accounting fees upfront — the first Profits Tax Return is typically due 18 months after incorporation, meaning you can keep working capital available for development.

What should a GetStarted.hk customer verify?

  • Which legal entity signs the engagement and provides each regulated or professional service.
  • Whether the relevant company-secretarial work is covered by an active TCSP arrangement. The Companies Registry TCSP search is a useful verification point.
  • Whether accounting, audit and tax filing are included, separately quoted or referred to another professional.
  • What the registered address covers, for how long and at what renewal price.
  • Whether banking assistance means document preparation, an introduction or another limited support function.

What does Statrys publish?

Statrys' official Hong Kong company-registration page presents an all-inclusive corporate-services package and a digital onboarding process. Its pricing page displays the incorporation package at HK$7,875 (promotional; listed full price HK$8,750) alongside pay-per-use accounting from HKD 891/month.

Published pricing is useful for building a shortlist, but it is not a complete first-year cost model. Confirm registered-address coverage, annual-return treatment, accounting period, tax-filing add-ons, audit arrangements, payment terms and the renewal price. If you need broader hands-on coordination, ask how support is escalated beyond the platform workflow.

What does Statrys' TCSP and MSO licensing tell you?

Statrys Corporate Services Limited holds TCSP licence TC008677, registered with the Companies Registry under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap 615). Statrys Limited also holds a Money Service Operator licence No. 19-02-02726 with Hong Kong Customs. These licences support the provider's position for both company-secretarial work and payment services, but neither guarantees banking approval or tax outcomes.

Founder pre-purchase checklist — six steps before paying either provider

Use this six-step checklist to convert the published material above into a decision you can defend to a co-founder, an investor or your own accountant.

Step 1 — Read each provider's published scope

Open GetStarted.hk's Hong Kong company-formation page and Statrys' Hong Kong company-registration page and pricing page side by side. Note the package names, listed inclusions and exclusions. Note the page date if shown.

Step 2 — Check TCSP licence status

Company secretarial work requires a Trust or Company Service Provider (TCSP) licence under Cap 615. Open the Companies Registry TCSP search, look up Statrys' licence (TC008677) and confirm an active licence is in place for GetStarted.hk's secretarial entity.

Step 3 — Request a dated written quotation

Email both providers and request a single dated quotation that lists: (a) government charges, (b) first-year professional fees, (c) registered-address scope and term, (d) annual return handling, (e) accounting, (f) tax filing, (g) audit coordination and (h) renewal pricing for year two onwards.

Step 4 — Map the workstreams to responsible professionals

For each workstream (formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax, banking assistance), identify the legal entity and the responsible professional. A separate regulated professional — for example a HK practising CPA — may handle the audit or tax filing. Ask each provider to confirm in writing.

Step 5 — Clarify banking and payment support boundaries

Ask each provider exactly what banking assistance covers — document preparation, an introduction to a relationship manager, ongoing liaison, or none of the above. A bank, neobank or payment provider makes its own approval decision; the provider cannot guarantee it.

Step 6 — Match the provider to your founder situation

If you want breadth of Hong Kong corporate-services coordination and a local relationship, GetStarted.hk may fit. If you prefer a structured digital-first workflow with an integrated multi-currency payment platform, Statrys may fit. Pause either choice if your company has complex cross-border tax, regulated activities, unusual ownership, high-risk jurisdictions or a difficult banking profile.

Which provider fits different founder situations?

Choose GetStarted.hk when breadth matters

  • You want formation and ongoing Hong Kong corporate-service coordination.
  • You expect to discuss secretarial work, accounting, audit, tax or banking support with a Hong Kong-focused provider.
  • You are prepared to obtain written confirmation of renewals, professional responsibility and what banking assistance actually means.

Choose Statrys when workflow structure matters

  • You prefer online onboarding and a digital-first service model.
  • You want to compare published package prices before speaking to a provider.
  • You are comfortable checking add-ons, promotional price states, renewal terms and the professionals responsible for accounting or audit work.

When should you pause before choosing either?

Pause if the company has complex cross-border tax, regulated activities, unusual ownership, high-risk jurisdictions, significant intellectual property, material audit issues or a difficult banking profile. A formation provider can help organise a process, but it cannot replace legal, tax, audit or regulated financial advice where those issues are material. For such situations, consult a HK practising solicitor, CPA or TCSP-licensed professional before signing.

What is the balanced verdict?

GetStarted.hk is the stronger initial shortlist for a founder who values breadth, local coordination and a wider Hong Kong corporate-services relationship. Statrys remains a credible alternative for a founder who prioritises a digital-first workflow, an integrated multi-currency business account and a clearly published package price. That is a conditional fit judgment, not a universal ranking.

The sensible decision is to compare like with like: first-year price, renewal price, government charges, company-secretarial responsibility, accounting and audit scope, tax filing, registered address, document handling and banking support. A provider should be able to explain those boundaries in writing before you pay.

For banking context, the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Scheme explains the separate role of deposit protection. It does not turn a company-services provider into a bank or guarantee an account. Likewise, a TCSP listing supports licence verification but does not guarantee a particular service experience.
