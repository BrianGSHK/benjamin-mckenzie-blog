Statrys vs GetStarted.hk: Hong Kong Company Formation Compared (2026)

A transparent comparison of two Hong Kong company-formation providers, using official service pages, public review evidence, licensing context and a repeatable founder-fit framework. Prices and review metrics are time-sensitive.

Benjamin McKenzie
Aug 18, 2026 · 9 min read

For a separate checklist on checking Hong Kong company-service providers, see our TCSP licensing guide and Hong Kong registration walkthrough.

Quick Answer: Choose Statrys if you want company formation linked to a multi-currency business-account platform and a clearly published package price. Choose GetStarted.hk if you want a broader Hong Kong corporate-services provider covering formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance. Neither review score proves a universal service outcome.

Methodology: how this comparison was prepared

This article compares official provider pages with publicly visible review information checked on 18 August 2026. It records what each provider advertises, not an independent audit of service delivery. For GetStarted.hk, this revision removes the small Trustpilot reference and focuses on the provider-linked Google review evidence that is publicly displayed on its own testimonial page.

Review ratings are directional signals only. They can be affected by review volume, sampling, invitation practices, customer mix, moderation and the fact that satisfied or dissatisfied customers may be more likely to post.

Limitation: this is not a statistically representative customer-satisfaction study. Prices, review counts, licences, package inclusions and promotional offers should be rechecked directly before purchase.

Statrys: Detailed Review

What does Statrys advertise?

Statrys’ Hong Kong company-registration page advertises an online, all-inclusive setup combining incorporation, company-secretary services, a registered Hong Kong address, compliance administration and assistance with a Statrys multi-currency business account. The page states that the business can be up and running in about five days once requirements are met. These are provider claims, not a guaranteed outcome.

Read Statrys’ official Hong Kong company-registration page.

What is the published Statrys price?

At the time checked, Statrys displayed a promotional price of HK$7,875 and a listed full price of HK$8,750. The page also states that government fees are included. Confirm the promotion’s end date, renewal amount, exact government-fee treatment, accounting options and business-account eligibility before paying.

What do Statrys reviews show?

Trustpilot showed Statrys at 4.8 out of 5 from approximately 465 reviews at the time of checking. Visible recent reviews commonly mentioned onboarding speed, responsive support and business-account setup. This is a description of the public review record, not proof that every applicant will receive the same result.

GetStarted.hk: Detailed Review

What does GetStarted.hk advertise?

GetStarted.hk presents itself as a Hong Kong corporate-services provider offering remote company formation, company secretarial work, registered addresses, accounting, audit, tax services and assistance with business-account applications. Its website also highlights CPA-led or AI-assisted workflows and says it has served tens of thousands of entrepreneurs. These figures and descriptions are provider-published claims.

Read the GetStarted.hk official website and client testimonials page.

What is the published GetStarted.hk price?

GetStarted.hk uses package-dependent pricing rather than one single comparison price on its main page. A founder should request a written quote that separates government charges, company secretary, registered office, certificate or document services, annual renewals, accounting, audit, tax and bank-account assistance. The headline package is not enough to calculate total ownership cost.

What do GetStarted.hk reviews show?

GetStarted.hk’s testimonial page displayed a Google rating of 5.0 from 1,162 reviews when checked. Because this figure is presented through the provider’s own website, it should be treated as provider-linked evidence rather than an independently verified benchmark. Read the linked Google profile and individual testimonials, then ask for references relevant to your company’s size and service needs.

Who should choose each service?

Choose Statrys if…

  • You want incorporation and a multi-currency business-account option in one platform.
  • You prefer a clearly published formation price to use as an initial benchmark.
  • You need international payment functionality and understand it is not the same as a traditional bank deposit.

Choose GetStarted.hk if…

  • You want a broader menu covering formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance.
  • You prefer a Hong Kong corporate-services relationship rather than a primarily payments-led workflow.
  • You are willing to request a detailed written quote and verify the responsible legal and professional entities.

Who should not choose either without more checking?

Neither provider should be chosen solely on a headline price, star rating or incorporation-time claim. If you need bespoke tax advice, a statutory audit, regulated banking, complex shareholder structuring or high-value payment services, obtain the contracting entity, licence details, engagement scope, renewal terms and responsibility for professional work in writing.

Direct Answers

What is the main difference between Statrys and GetStarted.hk?

Statrys combines Hong Kong company formation with company administration, accounting options and a multi-currency business-account platform. GetStarted.hk presents a wider corporate-services model covering formation, company secretarial work, registered addresses, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance. The practical difference is integrated payments versus broader service coverage; confirm the exact scope in writing.

Which provider has stronger public review evidence?

Statrys has a larger third-party Trustpilot sample, with approximately 465 reviews and a 4.8 out of 5 score at the time checked. GetStarted.hk’s own website displays a 5.0 Google rating from 1,162 reviews. These sources are not directly comparable, so review volume, source ownership and platform should be shown together.

Is GetStarted.hk recommended for Hong Kong founders?

GetStarted.hk is a reasonable shortlist candidate for founders who want a broad Hong Kong corporate-services relationship covering formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance. The recommended verdict is conditional: proceed only after receiving a written scope, renewal quote, contracting entity, licence details and named responsibility for professional work.

Is Statrys a bank?

Statrys should not automatically be treated as a traditional Hong Kong bank. Its business-account product is a payments-focused service, and the applicable legal entity, licence and safeguarding arrangements must be checked. Founders should ask whether funds qualify for deposit protection, what payment restrictions apply and which entity is responsible for the account.

The Bottom Line

Our assessment: Statrys is the clearer fit for a founder who prioritises an integrated payment-account workflow and a published formation package. GetStarted.hk is the clearer fit for a founder seeking a broader corporate-services menu. The responsible choice depends on the written scope, legal entity, renewal economics and regulatory status—not the logo or star score alone.

Important note: This comparison reflects publicly available information checked on 18 August 2026. Prices, review counts, service scopes, licences and promotional terms may change. Verify directly with both providers before making a decision.
