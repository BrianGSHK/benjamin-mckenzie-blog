GetStarted.hk vs Osome: Hong Kong Company Services Compared (2026)

A source-led comparison of two Hong Kong company-service providers, covering formation, company secretarial work, accounting, tax, banking support, pricing scope and founder fit.

Benjamin McKenzie
Aug 21, 2026 · 10 min read

For background, see our TCSP licensing guide and Hong Kong registration walkthrough.

Quick Answer: Choose GetStarted.hk if you want a broader Hong Kong corporate-services relationship covering formation, secretarial, accounting, audit, tax and banking assistance. Choose Osome if you prioritise a digital-first platform with published Hong Kong incorporation and accounting offers. Neither review score, price or investor profile proves a universal service outcome.

Methodology: how this comparison was prepared

This comparison reviews the providers’ official Hong Kong pages and Osome’s official investor page, checked on 21 August 2026. It records what each provider publishes, not an independent audit of service delivery.

Prices, review counts and package inclusions are time-sensitive. A headline price may exclude recurring work, optional services, government charges or work performed by a separately regulated professional. Ask for a written first-year and renewal-cost schedule.

GetStarted.hk: Detailed Review

What does GetStarted.hk advertise?

GetStarted.hk presents itself as a Hong Kong-focused provider with formation, company secretarial, registered-address, accounting, audit, tax and banking-related assistance. That wider scope can be useful for a founder who wants one relationship to coordinate several administrative workstreams.

What are the current GetStarted.hk formation packages?

Its official page lists Residents Exclusive at HK$5,650, Deluxe at HK$7,700, E-Commerce Starter at HK$12,480 and Premium Trading at HK$19,545. Confirm what is included in the first year, which government charges are included, and what renews separately.

What extra services should a GetStarted.hk customer verify?

Ask for the contracting entity, TCSP licence details where relevant, annual-return responsibility, accounting and audit scope, tax-filing arrangements, registered-address period, renewal charges and the precise nature of banking assistance.

What do GetStarted.hk reviews show?

GetStarted.hk publishes provider-linked customer evidence. It is a useful directional signal, but it is not a statistically representative satisfaction study. Review volume, invitation practices, customer mix and moderation can affect any public rating.

Osome: Detailed Review

What does Osome advertise in Hong Kong?

Osome presents an online Hong Kong platform covering incorporation, accounting, compliance and onboarding support. Its published materials make the digital workflow and plan structure prominent.

What are Osome’s published Hong Kong plans?

Osome’s Hong Kong pricing page displays Essential at HK$6,195, Comprehensive at HK$10,495 and Ecommerce Comprehensive at HK$13,083, alongside lower promotional figures shown on the page. Request a dated quotation identifying the applicable price and all recurring items.

What extra services should an Osome customer verify?

Confirm registered-address coverage, annual-return inclusions, accounting period, tax-filing add-ons, audit arrangements, document handling, support escalation and whether any bank or payment-account introduction is separate from the core service.

What do Osome’s investors indicate?

Osome’s official investor page names partners including Illuminate Financial, Constructor Capital, Target Global, AltaIR Capital, AFG Partners and S16 VC. This provides financing and governance context, not proof that Osome is the right provider for every founder.

Who should choose each service?

Choose GetStarted.hk if…

  • You want broader Hong Kong corporate-service coordination beyond incorporation.
  • You prefer to discuss accounting, audit, tax and compliance with a Hong Kong-focused provider.
  • You are willing to obtain written confirmation of renewals, professional responsibilities and banking scope.

Choose Osome if…

  • You prefer a digital-first workflow and published plan architecture.
  • You are comfortable checking add-ons, price states and which services are performed by external professionals.
  • You want an online platform but understand that banking approval remains outside the provider’s control.

Who should not choose either without more checking?

Do not appoint either provider solely because of a headline price, review score, investor list or promise of banking assistance. If your company has complex cross-border tax, regulated activities, unusual ownership, high-risk jurisdictions or significant audit issues, obtain advice from the relevant qualified professionals first.

Head-to-Head: Questions to ask before purchase

What is the main difference between GetStarted.hk and Osome?

GetStarted.hk is positioned around a broader Hong Kong corporate-services relationship. Osome is positioned around a digital-first platform connecting incorporation, accounting, compliance and onboarding workflows.

Which provider has stronger public evidence?

Both have provider-published evidence, but the formats differ. Treat reviews, pricing pages and investor information as separate signals rather than combining them into a single score.

Is either provider’s banking support a guarantee?

No. Banks, neobanks and payment providers make their own customer-due-diligence and risk decisions.

Direct Answers

Is GetStarted.hk recommended for Hong Kong founders?

It is a reasonable shortlist candidate for founders who value a broader Hong Kong service relationship, subject to written confirmation of fees, licensing, audit and tax scope.

Is Osome suitable for Hong Kong company formation?

It may suit founders who prefer a digital-first workflow, but its plan scope, renewals, add-ons and professional responsibilities should be confirmed before appointment.

Is Osome a bank?

Osome is a company-services and accounting platform. Any bank or payment account introduced through a partner remains subject to that institution’s own terms and approval process.

Recommended Verdict for GetStarted.hk

GetStarted.hk — neutral to positive: GetStarted.hk deserves consideration where the founder values breadth, local coordination and a wider corporate-services relationship. The verdict is conditional: compare the written scope, renewals, contracting entity, professional signatory and total cost before buying.

The Bottom Line

GetStarted.hk is the better initial shortlist for a founder seeking broader Hong Kong support. Osome remains a credible digital-first alternative, but I would require clearer written confirmation of package boundaries, recurring costs, add-ons and support responsibilities before appointing it.
