Hong Kong Corporate Bank Account Approval Times for Non-Residents

An anonymised descriptive report on corporate-account processing times and approval rates across five applicant groups.

By Benjamin McKenzie · Published 13 August 2026 · Data source: supplied anonymised workbook
Direct answerThe supplied anonymised dataset represents 805 corporate-account application attempts across 96 company records. The application-weighted mean processing time was approximately 21.7 days and the application-weighted approval rate was approximately 74.7%. This is descriptive data, not a guarantee of approval or processing time.

What does the anonymised dataset show?

The workbook contains corporate-account records only. It includes five country-of-residence groups: India, Mainland China, Singapore, the United Kingdom and the United States. Company names were excluded from this report. The source workbook does not specify a reporting quarter, so this page does not label the results Q2 or Q3 2026.

How were the statistics calculated?

The application-weighted mean days equals the sum of each record's mean processing days multiplied by its application count, divided by the total application count. The application-weighted approval rate applies the same application-count weighting to each record's supplied approval rate.

The “median of record medians” is the median of the company-level median fields supplied in the workbook. It is not a pooled median of all 805 individual applications, because the underlying individual-day observations were not supplied.

What are the key takeaways?

The overall weighted approval rate was approximately 74.7%, and the overall weighted mean processing time was approximately 21.7 days. The United States group recorded the highest weighted approval rate at 84.1% and the shortest weighted mean at 15.5 days. India recorded the lowest weighted approval rate at 64.9% in this dataset.

These descriptive differences should not be interpreted as proof that country of residence alone determines bank outcomes. Business model, ownership, source of funds, expected transactions, sector, documentation and each bank's internal policies may affect the result.

For regulatory context rather than an approval prediction, readers can review the Hong Kong Monetary Authority deposits information and its digital banks overview. These official pages explain the banking environment but do not validate the anonymised figures in this report.

Does this report rank individual banks?

No. The workbook identifies banks applied to, but the supplied data does not provide reliable bank-level application outcomes. This report therefore does not rank HSBC, BOC, Hang Seng, BEA, Citibank or DBS, and it does not imply that any particular bank will approve or reject an application.

Data limitations

  • The reporting period is not specified in the supplied workbook.
  • The dataset contains corporate accounts only, not personal accounts.
  • Approval rates are supplied at record level and cannot be independently reconstructed from individual outcomes.
  • Processing times are not a promised service level.
  • The data does not establish causation or nationality-based treatment.
  • Bank requirements and onboarding policies can change.
