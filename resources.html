Original founder resources

Practical tools for Hong Kong founders

Choose a focused tool for company formation, tax planning, compliance, banking or provider checks. These resources are educational references; confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified adviser.

How to use these tools

Choose the tool that matches the decision in front of you, record the assumptions you used, and keep the result with your working papers. These are educational resources, not official filings or professional advice. Confirm current requirements with the relevant Hong Kong authority before acting.

Which tool should I start with?

Use the formation timeline before incorporation, the HSIC search when describing a business activity, the banking comparison before account applications, the compliance checklist for recurring duties, and the calculators only for illustrative estimates.

Can these tools determine my legal or tax position?

No. They help organise questions and evidence. A company’s obligations depend on its facts, accounting records, activities, ownership, transactions and the current rules.
