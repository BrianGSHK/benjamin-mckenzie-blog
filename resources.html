Practical tools for Hong Kong founders

These resources turn the most common setup questions into clear working references. They are educational tools, not legal or tax advice. Always confirm current requirements with the relevant Hong Kong authority or a qualified adviser.

HK company formation timeline

  1. Choose the structure. Most founders compare a private company limited by shares with a sole proprietorship before preparing documents.
  2. Prepare identity and incorporation information. Confirm the proposed name, registered office, company secretary, directors and shareholders.
  3. File with Companies Registry. The official incorporation process and forms are described by the Companies Registry.
  4. Complete business registration and operating setup. Arrange accounting records, banking, licences and internal controls based on the business.
  5. Plan the first compliance calendar. Record annual return, business registration renewal, accounting and tax milestones.

Official reference: Companies Registry — Register a Company.

HK company compliance checklist

  • ☐ Keep the registered office and company secretary details current.
  • ☐ Maintain accounting records and supporting documents.
  • ☐ Track the annual return filing date.
  • ☐ Renew the business registration when due.
  • ☐ Review whether an audit and profits tax filing are required.
  • ☐ Keep director, shareholder and beneficial-owner records updated.
  • ☐ Review licences and sector-specific permissions before trading.

Useful references: annual returns and business registration renewal.

TCSP licensing guide

A company providing trust or company services in Hong Kong may need a TCSP licence. Before engaging a provider, check its legal name, licence status, scope of service and the identity of the contracting entity.

  1. Search the official TCSP register.
  2. Match the provider's legal entity and business address.
  3. Ask what is included: company secretary, registered office, filings and compliance reminders.
  4. Check renewal terms, fees, data handling and termination arrangements.

Verify providers using the Companies Registry TCSP licence search.
