Registration typically takes 1-3 business days via the Companies Registry. Preparing documents and opening a bank account may take 1-2 additional weeks.

Hong Kong companies must file Annual Returns with the Companies Registry, prepare financial statements, conduct an audit if applicable, and file a Profits Tax Return with the IRD.

Basic setup costs range from HK$5,000 to HK$15,000, including government fees, company secretary, and registered address. Annual maintenance is typically HK$8,000 to HK$20,000.
