Hong Kong company compliance checklist

Use this checklist as a recurring review. The exact obligations depend on the company, its activities, accounting period and applicable Hong Kong requirements.

  • ☐ Keep registered office and company secretary details current.
  • ☐ Maintain accounting records and supporting documents.
  • ☐ Track the annual return filing date.
  • ☐ Renew business registration when due.
  • ☐ Review audit and profits tax filing obligations.
  • ☐ Keep director, shareholder and beneficial-owner records updated.
  • ☐ Review licences before starting regulated activities.

References: annual returns and business registration renewal.
