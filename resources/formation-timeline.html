Hong Kong company formation timeline

Use this sequence to organise the main decisions and filings before a Hong Kong company begins operating. A straightforward application can move quickly when the name, ownership, identity documents and business purpose are ready; regulated activities, non-resident ownership or unresolved due diligence can add time.

What is the usual order for forming a Hong Kong company?

Most founders first choose the legal structure and name, confirm the director, shareholder, registered office and company secretary details, prepare the incorporation documents, submit the application with the business-registration notice, then set up banking, records, licences and the first compliance calendar. The order is practical rather than a guaranteed processing timetable.

Formation timeline at a glance

  1. Choose the structure and business purpose.

    For many operating businesses, a private company limited by shares is the starting point. Check whether a branch, company limited by guarantee or another structure better matches the activity, ownership and liability position. A company name search is available through the Companies Registry e-Services Portal.

  2. Confirm the people and registered details.

    Prepare the proposed name, registered office in Hong Kong, company secretary, director(s), shareholder(s), share capital and business activity. A non-Hong Kong resident can be a director or shareholder, but the company still needs the required Hong Kong registered office and company-secretary arrangements.

  3. Prepare and file the incorporation application.

    For a local company limited by shares, the Companies Registry identifies Form NNC1 and the accompanying IRBR1 notice. Check the current form, filing route and fee directly before submission; incomplete identity or ownership information can delay review.

  4. Receive the certificates and organise the operating file.

    Keep the Certificate of Incorporation, Business Registration Certificate, articles, company particulars and beneficial-ownership records together. Before trading, arrange accounting records, a business bank-account application, insurance where relevant and any sector-specific licence or approval.

  5. Create the first compliance calendar.

    Record the anniversary date, annual-return deadline, business-registration renewal, accounting close, audit planning and Profits Tax Return workflow. A local private company generally delivers its annual return within 42 days after the anniversary of incorporation; verify the current requirement for the company’s exact status.

What should a founder prepare before filing?

  • Two or more proposed company names, checked against the Companies Registry search.
  • Identity and address information for directors and shareholders, with extra time allowed for non-resident or corporate owners.
  • The Hong Kong registered office and company-secretary details.
  • A plain-English description of the business activity and any likely regulated sectors.
  • Ownership, share allocation and source-of-funds information that can be explained consistently to the incorporation provider and bank.
  • A decision on who will maintain accounting, tax, annual-return and licence records after incorporation.

What can change the timing?

Usually faster

One clear ownership structure, an available name, complete identity documents and a business activity that does not require a separate licence.

Often needs more review

Corporate shareholders, complex ownership, non-resident applicants, inconsistent documents, a restricted name or an activity subject to licensing or enhanced due diligence.

Official references: Companies Registry — Register a Company, specified forms including NNC1 and IRBR1, and annual-return requirements for local private companies.

How to use this timeline

Use the sequence to decide what information to prepare before incorporation. The timing is not a promise of Companies Registry processing time: identity checks, document readiness, the business model and any licence requirements can change the sequence.

What should I prepare first?

Confirm the proposed name, shareholders, directors, registered office, company secretary, business activity and ownership information before requesting a quotation or filing.

What does this timeline not decide?

It does not decide whether a structure is tax-efficient, whether a bank will approve an account or whether a regulated activity is permitted.
