My Journey

Growing up in Scotland, I developed a passion for understanding how businesses work. This led me to study at the University of Cambridge, where I gained a strong foundation in business and finance.

After graduating, I moved to Hong Kong over a decade ago, drawn by the city's dynamic energy and its position as a gateway to Asia. I spent several years working in venture capital and private equity, evaluating hundreds of startups and growth-stage companies across the region.

Through this experience, I saw firsthand how many promising founders struggled with the administrative and regulatory complexities of company formation. I realized I could make a bigger impact by helping entrepreneurs get their foundations right from the start.

Today, I focus on demystifying company formation, compliance, and business setup for founders who want to build with confidence. Whether you're a first-time entrepreneur or an experienced founder expanding into Asia, I'm here to help you navigate the process with clarity.
