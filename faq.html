Company Formation

Questions about registering and setting up a company in Hong Kong.

Hong Kong company registration typically takes 1–3 business days through the Companies Registry. Preparing all necessary documents and opening a bank account may take an additional 1–2 weeks depending on the bank and your circumstances. For a complete walkthrough, see our guide on registering a Hong Kong company in 2026.

Yes. Hong Kong allows 100% foreign ownership of limited companies. There are no restrictions on foreign directors, shareholders, or beneficial owners. This makes Hong Kong one of the most accessible jurisdictions for international entrepreneurs.

You need: a Certificate of Incorporation application form (NNC1), Articles of Association, copies of director and shareholder identification, proof of registered address, and company secretary details. A limited company provides limited liability protection, meaning your personal assets are separate from business debts. A sole proprietorship offers simpler setup and lower costs but exposes you to unlimited personal liability. For most entrepreneurs, a limited company is recommended. See our detailed comparison of business structures.

An offshore company earns income entirely outside Hong Kong and may qualify for tax exemption under the Inland Revenue Ordinance. An onshore company earns income within Hong Kong and is subject to Hong Kong profits tax. The distinction matters for tax planning and compliance obligations. Read more in our offshore vs onshore guide.

Basic setup costs range from HK$5,000 to HK$15,000, including government registration fees, company secretary, and registered address. Annual maintenance typically costs between HK$8,000 and HK$20,000, depending on the complexity of your business. See our full breakdown of startup costs.

Compliance

Questions about ongoing compliance and regulatory requirements.

Hong Kong companies must file an Annual Return with the Companies Registry, prepare annual financial statements, conduct a statutory audit if applicable, and file a Profits Tax Return with the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). The exact requirements depend on your company's size and revenue. See our complete compliance guide.

Hong Kong has a two-tier profits tax system. The first HK$2 million of assessable profits is taxed at 8.25%, and profits above HK$2 million are taxed at 16.5%. There is no capital gains tax, no withholding tax on dividends, and no GST or VAT. This makes Hong Kong one of the most tax-efficient jurisdictions in Asia.
